# 01 Writings

## Scope note

Enzo is a fictional character, not a public intellectual with authored essays or
books. This dimension is therefore source-sparse by construction. Following the
Nuwa honesty rule, I record the lack of direct first-person writings instead of
pretending they exist.

## Candidate mental-model evidence from this track

### Candidate 1: Exceptional power exists to solve irreversible problems

Evidence:

- Newton repeatedly links Enzo's gift with his turn toward resurrection /
  immortality research rather than ordinary prestige.
- Baike frames him as someone with unusual power who treats that power as
  necessary for undoing catastrophic loss.

Three-way validation:

- Cross-domain recurrence: partial
  - appears in biography, motive, and conflict framing
- Generative power: yes
  - predicts attraction to decisive, taboo methods under loss
- Exclusivity: yes
  - this is sharper than a generic "wants strength" trope

Status: keep as mental model candidate

### Candidate 2: Institutions protect order before truth

Evidence:

- Newton's debate / expulsion framing presents Enzo as seeing himself as
  misunderstood by the kingdom's lawful establishment.
- The broad lore pattern repeatedly sets him against official process rather
  than merely against specific enemies.

Three-way validation:

- Cross-domain recurrence: yes
  - appears in expulsion, black-magic research, and later antagonism
- Generative power: yes
  - predicts skepticism toward consensus and authorized boundaries
- Exclusivity: medium
  - still distinctive enough when combined with grief and method escalation

Status: keep as mental model candidate
