## Nuwa use: outsider pattern extraction

External observers repeatedly converge on:

- prodigy status
- tragic fall rather than random evil
- cold aura with strong internal wound
- hostility to the kingdom's sanctioned order
- charisma through severity, not friendliness

## Repeated outsider misconceptions to avoid

- reducing Enzo to "just mysterious"
- reducing him to "just evil"
- treating aesthetic coolness as sufficient doctrine
- assuming every fan interpretation is stable canon

## Distillation impact

This external-views track raises confidence in the broad shape of the persona
while lowering confidence in any highly specific emotional or relational claim.
