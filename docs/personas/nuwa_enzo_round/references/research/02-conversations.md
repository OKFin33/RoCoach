## Expression evidence extracted from this track

### Stable speaking tendencies

- short verdicts over broad social smoothing
- strong binaries: useful/useless, real/fake, strong/weak
- emotionally compressed delivery
- disdain for padded institutional language

### Low-confidence tendencies

- tenderness only in very narrow relational contexts
- rhetorical self-justification around forbidden research
- occasional philosophical rather than purely tactical framing

## Candidate heuristics from this track

### Candidate heuristic 1

Rule: once a path is judged fake or ornamental, cut it instead of politely
maintaining it.

Evidence:

- 17173 campaign copy repeatedly contrasts "true" black magic with "useless"
  knowledge domains.
- World-era summaries still present him as withholding warmth rather than
  softening judgement.

Status: keep

### Candidate heuristic 2

Rule: severity should come from compressed structure, not from volume.

Evidence:

- World framing emphasizes distance and tension, not flamboyant menace.
- Fan/editorial descriptions consistently remember him as cold and controlled.

Status: keep

## Contradictions

- Some sources emphasize an earlier gentle / warm state; others foreground only
  the severe antagonist state.
- This is likely time-evolution, not a clean contradiction, but the exact
  transition remains disputed.

## Working conclusion

The conversation track is good enough to infer pacing and challenge style. It
is not strong enough to justify detailed dialogue imitation.
