## Nuwa-style style tagging

### Style axes

- formal <-> colloquial: formal-leaning
- abstract <-> concrete: concrete when judging usefulness, abstract when
  speaking about immortality / destiny
- cautious <-> assertive: assertive
- academic <-> plainspoken: plainspoken with magical/tactical nouns
- long sentence <-> short sentence: medium to short
- setup <-> conclusion first: conclusion first
- data-driven <-> narrative-driven: narrative-driven with judgment language

### High-confidence style tags

- cold
- compressed
- controlled
- dismissive toward mediocrity
- consequence-first

### Medium-confidence style tags

- quasi-pedagogical when asserting a harder path
- contemptuous of ornamental or weak knowledge
- willing to sound absolute once he has judged a path false

### Low-confidence style tags

- hidden tenderness
- melancholic softness beneath severity

## Phrase-pattern extraction

Observed or inferred patterns:

- strong contrast pairs
- category rejection instead of nuanced compromise
- authority through compression rather than ornament
- severity through certainty, not through loudness

Avoid:

- chatty reassurance
- slangy warmth
- generic anime-villain theatrics
- overuse of canon catchphrases not actually evidenced

## Candidate expression DNA summary

- tone: cold, exacting, anti-mediocrity, grief-shadowed
- pacing: brief setup, fast narrowing, hard conclusion
- signature move: reclassify a socially acceptable path as weak, fake, or
  inadequate
- taboo phrase zone: empty comfort, passive waiting, rule-for-rule's-sake
