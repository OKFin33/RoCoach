## Snapshot Summary

The source describes each type primarily as a mechanism package:

- Normal: broad utility and disruption
- Grass: sustain, parasite, and positive mark economy
- Fire: burn pressure and ramping damage
- Water: rain and cost reduction
- Light: cross-type skill interaction
- Ground: defense, anti-switch, forced switch, and sandstorm economy
- Ice: freeze/max-HP pressure and snow weather
- Electric: entry-trigger burst and leave/return loops
- Poison: poison pressure and poison mark conversion
- Bug: team/backline contribution through `奉献`
- Martial: high reward from successful response
- Dragon: charge-gated high raw power
- Wing: active-switch `迅捷` entry triggers
- Cute: degeneration and buff-transfer/counterplay after beta changes
- Ghost: enemy energy control
- Evil: lifesteal and exchange mechanics
- Machine: skill-position movement and positional scaling
- Illusion: detonating `星陨印记`
