## One-Line Goal

Design a Karpathy-style, source-controlled, LLM-maintained Battle Wiki for
Roco's generic PvP doctrine layer:

```text
A = structured facts / Engine / battle-dex
B = generic battle doctrine wiki
Persona = pluggable style or persona-specific overlay
```

## Hard Boundaries

- Roco is about `洛克王国：世界` PvP 精灵对战, not Pokemon.
- Pokemon-derived vocabulary may only be used as explicitly approved analogy,
  never as imported mechanics.
- B layer must remain generic and persona-free.
- Enzo or any other persona belongs to the persona layer, not the default B
  doctrine layer.
- The wiki must preserve provenance, confidence, and update history.
- The Battle Wiki is for doctrine, methodology, tactical taste, and cases; it
  must not duplicate A-layer exact facts as a second source of truth.
