# Battle Wiki Confidence Policy

Allowed confidence values:

- `confirmed`
- `provisional`
- `low_confidence`
- `deprecated`

## confirmed

Use only when the claim has strong project evidence, direct PM review, or
stable support from approved Roco project documents.

Confirmed doctrine may guide default synthesis, but still may not override
A-layer facts.

## provisional

Use when the claim is useful for explanation or reasoning but still needs more
evidence.

Provisional doctrine may guide soft recommendations and tradeoff framing.

## low_confidence

Use for weak community claims, early tactical speculation, or unverified pattern
observations.

Low-confidence doctrine must not drive hard recommendations by default.

## deprecated

Use for rejected, stale, or superseded claims preserved for drift history or
counterexample value.

Deprecated doctrine must not enter compiled default runtime context.
