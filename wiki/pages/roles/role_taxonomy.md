# Role Taxonomy

## Claim

Role is not a species label. Role is a team-contextual job created by selected
set, trait, speed, bulk, energy profile, mark/weather access, and matchup plan.

The same species may be evaluated differently in different teams.

## Strategic Use

Useful first-pass roles:

- primary damage core: converts setup into KOs or decisive pressure
- secondary damage core: pressures when the main core is denied
- support/enabler: creates marks, weather, buffs, energy, or entry windows
- defensive pivot: absorbs key attacks and preserves team tempo
- counterpiece: exists to punish a specific common plan
- disruption piece: attacks energy, switching, marks, speed, or action order
- cleaner: enters after conditions are prepared to finish the game

The advisor should always state the role and the reason:

```text
This member is a support/enabler here because it starts the resource engine and
protects the later cleaner, not because its species is always a support.
```

## Evidence

Champion-team material describes output, support, and interception/counter
roles. The archetype deck shows that teams often assign narrow jobs: stored
mark burst converter, poison pressure protector, response pressure piece,
energy disruptor, or scaling core support.

## A-Layer Boundary

This page does not decide if a species can perform a role. A-layer data must
verify stats, speed, move access, trait, typing, and energy costs.

## Known Failure Modes

- Calling a species a role without checking the selected set.
- Treating a rating list as a role taxonomy.
- Ignoring whether the team already has the same role covered.
- Ignoring whether a support action can be safely executed.
